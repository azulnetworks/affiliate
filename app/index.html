Common questions

Are the walks self-guided?
Yes. Every walk comes with GPS-guided directions, photos of each stop, and written commentary from your local guide. You go at your own pace — no group, no schedule.
How long does a typical walk take?
Most walks are between 1.5 and 3 hours, depending on how long you linger at each stop. Every route shows estimated time and distance before you start.
Do I need mobile data?
No. Download the walk before you leave your hotel and everything works offline — maps, directions, photos, and tips. GPS works without data.
Is Wanderly free?
You can preview every walk for free. Full access to GPS directions and guide commentary starts at $3.99 per walk, or unlimited with Wanderly Pro for $4.99/month.
Who creates the walks?
Every walk is created by a verified local guide — people who live in the city and know their neighborhood intimately. We vet every guide and walk each route ourselves before publishing.
